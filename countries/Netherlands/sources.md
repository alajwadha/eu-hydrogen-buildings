## External

**Eurostat (H1 2025):** Gas nrg_pc_202; Electricity nrg_pc_204
**Eurostat (2023):** NL 72.9% residential space heating from gas — highest in EU
**Hotmaps, EMBER 2024, EEA Fit-for-55** as standard

**HPT (Heat Pumping Technologies) Magazine 2025:**
- 700,000 individual HPs installed in NL (1 in 12 homes) as of 2024
- 75% HP share in new builds; 13–24% in gas-boiler replacements
- 30% market decline in 2024 due to policy uncertainty
- https://heatpumpingtechnologies.org/articles/heat-pumping-technologies-magazine-vol-43-no-1-2025/national-market-report-italy/ (article titled NL despite URL)

**EHPA 2024:**
- Dutch HP industry response to cancellation of 2026 legislation
- https://ehpa.org/news-and-resources/press-releases/dutch-heat-pump-industry-responds-to-cancellation-of-2026-legislation/

**PV Magazine 2023:**
- Hybrid HP mandate exemptions for apartments + monuments
- https://www.pv-magazine.com/2023/05/03/netherlands-exempts-apartments-and-monuments-from-2026-heat-pump-mandate/

**HomeBuilding & Renovating 2022:**
- Original gas-boiler-ban announcement
- €150M/yr subsidy through 2030

**EIBI 2022:**
- Wet Voortgang Energietransitie: gas-free new builds from 2018
- https://eibi.co.uk/news/the-netherlands-to-ban-fossil-fuel-heating-from-2026/

**ABN AMRO Research:**
- Hybrid HP scaling targets, 2M units by 2030 originally

**Homekeur 2025:**
- Confirmation of 2026 mandate cancellation by new government

**REN21 Global Status Report 2024 — Netherlands snapshot:**
- 2018 ban on new gas connections; hybrid HP minimum standard agreed 2021

**Netbeheer Nederland (DSO association):**
- HR-Hybrid Coalition plan
- Action Plan Hybrid Heat Pumps 2022–2024

**Gasunie:**
- H₂ network plans (Hynet, HyTransPort)
