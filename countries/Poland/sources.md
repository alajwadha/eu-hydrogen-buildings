## External

**Eurostat (H1 2025):** Gas nrg_pc_202; Electricity nrg_pc_204
**Hotmaps, EMBER 2024, EEA Fit-for-55** as standard

**IEA Clean Air Programme policy page:**
- Czyste Powietrze grant levels: up to PLN 30,000 basic / 37,000 enhanced / +5k for PV
- https://www.iea.org/policies/11538-clean-air-programme

**European Commission — Update of Clean Air Priority Programme:**
- Programme reform 2024
- https://commission.europa.eu/projects/update-clean-air-priority-programme_en

**World Bank Poland Clean Air Programme analyses:**
- 85% of single-family buildings rely on solid fuels (coal/biomass)
- ~2M+ polluting boilers
- PLN 22.5bn budget (~€5bn) over 2018–2029
- Boiler replacement under CAPP: 42% condensing gas, 23% pellet, 18% HP, 15% eco-coal (until 2020)
- https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/431341641931737643/

**Climate Change Laws of the World:**
- Clean Air Programme up to PLN 135,000 / PLN 99,000 / PLN 66,000 by funding tier
- https://climate-laws.org/document/clean-air-programme-czyste-powietrze_38f5

**Notes from Poland (April 2024):**
- World Bank lent €250M for Clean Air; total programme budget over $25bn

**Trade.gov (June 2024):**
- World Bank $266M lending agreement for Clean Air

**Besteon (Nov 2024):**
- Czyste Powietrze, Moje Ciepło, Mój Prąd subsidy comparison
- Grant levels and changes

**Clean Air Fund (2025):**
- Health/air quality impact of solid fuel boilers
- Poland 48% coal dependent, 25% gas in energy mix
- https://www.cleanairfund.org/news-item/polands-renovation-revolution/

**Successful Investing in Poland (April 2025):**
- 2025 Czyste Powietrze reform: EU/EFTA-only manufacturing requirement
- https://successful-investing-in-poland.com/clean-air-programme-only-for-companies-from-europe-what-does-it-mean-for-investors/

**GUS (Statistics Poland):**
- ~14.5M dwellings nationally
- Half single-family

**Forum Energii / Instrat reports:**
- Poland coal exit timelines
- Heat pump market reports
