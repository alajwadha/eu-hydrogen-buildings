## External

**Eurostat / Swiss Federal Office of Energy (BFE/SFOE):** Gas and electricity prices H1 2025

**EMBER 2024 / EEA Fit-for-55 / IEA Switzerland 2023:** Grid CO₂

**SFOE (Swiss Federal Office of Energy):**
- Energy Strategy 2050
- Energy Perspectives 2050+ (EP2050+)
- Building Programme (Gebäudeprogramm) data

**EnDK (Conference of Cantonal Energy Directors):**
- MuKEn 2014, MuKEn 2025 draft regulations
- Study on quasi-ban cantons (~90% renewable heating in new installations)

**UBS Switzerland (2024 article):**
- Heating system replacement costs (CHF 27k typical HP retrofit, up to CHF 60k)
- Cantonal regulation summary
- MuKEn enforcement mechanism
- https://www.ubs.com/ch/en/services/guide/mortgages-and-financing/articles/heating-replacement-tips.html

**PLOS Climate (2026):**
- *Decarbonising existing buildings with heat pumps – Early evidence and policy analysis for Switzerland*
- LCOH comparison: ASHP 16% above gas without subsidy; subsidy makes equivalent
- Quasi-ban cantons effectiveness analysis
- https://journals.plos.org/climate/article?id=10.1371/journal.pclm.0000608

**Renowave 2024:**
- *Policies for accelerating fossil heating systems replacement and renewable heating system adoption in Switzerland*
- MuKEn 2025 policy options
- https://www.renowave.ch/pdf/180050-Accelerating_fossil_heater_replacements.pdf

**SWI / SwissInfo 2024:**
- 2024 HP sales decline (-47% H1), gas/oil installs rise (+12%)
- https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/climate-solutions/more-heat-pumps-and-less-oil-how-the-swiss-heat-their-homes/88226331

**LeNews 2024:**
- HP cost outlier analysis: Swiss vs Swedish ground-source HP cost differences
- https://lenews.ch/2024/11/29/swiss-homeowners-shun-heat-pumps-in-favour-of-gas-and-oil-heating/

**Val Index 2026:**
- ~3,200 HVAC firms in CH
- ~450,000 HPs in operation, 42,000/year installations
- 60% of buildings still on fossil fuels
- CHF 360M+ Gebäudeprogramm 2024
- https://valindex.ch/en/industry/hvac-green-energy/

**myclimate (Swiss climate non-profit):**
- HP support programme data: 10.7 TWh renewable heat in 2024
- Multi-dwelling support programme

**Climate and Innovation Act (2023):**
- Swiss federal climate law approved by referendum June 2023
- CHF 200M/year for fossil heating replacement through 2030

**Trutnevyte et al. studies (PLOS Climate):**
- Agent-based policy assessment for HP adoption in Switzerland
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301421525002253

**BFS (Federal Statistical Office):**
- ~4.7M dwellings; 70% multi-family

**OIES ET08 / ET32 (for RQ2 H₂ import prices):**
- ET08 (2022): H₂ import pricing methodology
- ET32 (2024): Updated EU H₂ import landscape
