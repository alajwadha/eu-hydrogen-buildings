## External

**Eurostat (H1 2025):** Gas nrg_pc_202 (€213 — outlier); Electricity nrg_pc_204
**Hotmaps, EMBER 2024, EEA Fit-for-55** as standard

**IEA Bioenergy Sweden Country Report 2024:**
- DH = 36% of final energy demand in residential sector
- Three-quarters of DH from biomass
- Carbon tax history (1991)
- Fossil fuels <5% in residential sector
- https://www.ieabioenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/CountryReport2024_Sweden_final.pdf

**EHPA Roundtable 2024:**
- DH ~50% of Swedish heating; 60% of buildings (2015)
- Heat pumps + DH synergy
- https://ehpa.org/news-and-resources/news/hej-ren-energi-swedish-perspectives-on-clean-heating-and-cooling/

**SEI Policy Brief (Dzebo & Nykvist 2017):**
- Swedish heat energy transition history
- DH 50% of heating in building stock
- 75% of heating from DH+electricity; <5% fossil
- https://www.sei.org/publications/swedish-heat-energy-system-new-tensions-and-lock-ins-after-a-successful-transition/
- https://mediamanager.sei.org/documents/Publications/SEI-2017-PB-Dzebo-Nykvist-SweHeatEnergySystem-eng.pdf

**Wikipedia / Energiföretagen Sverige 2024:**
- 2024 DH fuel mix: 46% biomass, 22% waste-to-energy, 11% flue-gas, 8% industrial waste heat, 7% large HPs, 3% grid electricity, 2% fossil, 0.3% peat

**Mordor Intelligence Sweden Heat Pump Market 2024:**
- SEK 30,000 grant + 50% ROT deduction = 40–60% cost reduction
- Air-source 78% of market; 62% sub-10 kW
- $364.7M market in 2024, 3.0% CAGR
- https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/sweden-heat-pump-market

**Araner technical analysis:**
- Swedish DH heat pump integration trends
- Biomass historical advantage, transition direction
- https://www.araner.com/blog/district-heating-in-sweden-efficiency-innovation

**ScienceDirect — 5GDHC in Sweden (2023):**
- 500 DH systems, 23,000 km of pipes
- DH supplies 60% of heating in buildings
- Sankey diagram of Swedish DH 2020: 45% biomass, 39% waste, electricity for large HPs

**ScienceDirect — Biomass DH history (2016):**
- Two-thirds of DH from biomass + waste; biomass alone ~50%
- Policy drivers behind Sweden's biomass transition

**GEA case study (Malmö):**
- 4× 10 MW ammonia HPs in Malmö DH; sewage + waste incineration heat sources

**SCB (Statistics Sweden):**
- ~4.9M dwellings nationally
