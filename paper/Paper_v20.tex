% No page may open or close on one or two stray lines of someone else's paragraph. The
% LaTeX default is 150, a price TeX pays readily, so eight pages opened on the one or two
% lines a paragraph had left over.
%
% \widowpenalty on its own forbids a single line only. The eTeX \widowpenalties form takes
% one penalty per depth, so depths one and two are forbidden outright here. Depth three is
% left at the default deliberately: forbidding it too makes every paragraph of four to six
% lines effectively unbreakable, and the document grew by twenty pages when it was set.
% As it stands the cost is two pages and six of the eight are gone. The two that remain
% are reference entries carrying long URLs, where the alternative break is worse.
\widowpenalties=3 10000 10000 150
\clubpenalties=3 10000 10000 150
\displaywidowpenalties=3 10000 10000 150
\brokenpenalty=10000
% Forbidding a break is only half of it, because TeX still has to put the displaced lines

% new one, and 3 pt of stretch on a one-and-a-half spaced page does not show.

\section*{Data Availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Data Availability}
The model and the underlying data are available from the authors on reasonable request.
Enquiries should be directed to the corresponding author.

The supplementary material referred to in the text comprises the scenario parameter
register of per-country envelope rates and scenario levers; the derivation of the
renovation depth-band weights; the full per-country and per-scenario missing-money table;
the capacity-stack data note; the Swiss fixed-point iteration history; the per-country
delivered hydrogen supply costs; the source audit behind the techno-economic inputs; the
per-country energy against dwelling-share diagnostic; the ground-source per-ceiling
sweep; the scope-2-inclusive emissions accounting; and the per-country cost-optimal
shares and shadow prices. These are available on the same basis.
